# Week 01 content design

## Purpose

Week 01 will establish HireNudge as a credible source of decision support for Indian freshers considering global remote and contractor roles. The primary objective is audience trust and authority. Qualified HireNudge product interest is secondary; reach and follower growth are supporting outcomes.

This specification defines the research, selection and channel-planning system. It does not approve a topic, hook, script, caption or product claim.

## Current operational state

- The native Google Sheet is the operational index, and the linked Google Docs hold research and production detail.
- The Week 01 Research and Selection Doc remains unfilled and has no selected option.
- All 22 registered assets remain in `Research backlog`.
- No Parent Pack ID, Concept Key or Hook Key has been assigned.
- The production briefs contain structure only.

## Audience and hiring route

- Primary audience: a broad mix of Indian tech and non-tech final-year students, recent graduates and freshers.
- Hiring-route focus: global remote and contractor work that an applicant based in India may realistically consider.
- The content must distinguish worldwide remote work from country-limited remote work and must not imply eligibility when the source does not establish it.

## Evidence inputs

Research must produce three candidates before selection:

1. A currently live global remote or contractor role.
2. A second currently live global remote or contractor role with a meaningfully different applicant decision.
3. Verified market data or a named recruiter observation relevant to freshers pursuing global remote or contractor work.

Each candidate must record:

- source links and check date;
- intended audience;
- employment or contractor route;
- country, location and eligibility restrictions;
- the central applicant decision;
- why the input is useful now;
- material risks or caveats; and
- a proposed Concept Key.

Do not invent a vacancy, recruiter quote, outcome, product capability, work-authorisation rule or contractor implication.

## Selection method

The three candidates will be evaluated on five dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight | Evaluation question |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Evidence strength | 30% | Is the source primary, current and sufficiently complete? |
| Applicant-decision value | 25% | Does the input help a fresher make a specific, consequential choice? |
| Route and eligibility clarity | 20% | Can the remote or contractor route and its restrictions be explained accurately? |
| Freshness | 15% | Is the input timely enough for Week 01 production and publication? |
| Cross-channel usefulness | 10% | Can the same central decision support genuinely native treatments on all four channels? |

A candidate fails regardless of score if its central claim cannot be sourced, the role's availability or eligibility cannot be checked, consent is missing for a case or contribution, or the treatment would require individual legal, tax, visa or employment advice.

Priyansh selects one candidate after the comparison is complete. Channel content must not be drafted before this selection.

## Pack and duplicate controls

The selected candidate receives one Parent Pack ID and one Concept Key shared by all Week 01 adaptations. Every produced asset receives a distinct Hook Key.

The sequence is:

1. Keep all assets in `Research backlog` while the three options are being completed.
2. Review the three options and record the selected candidate.
3. Assign the Parent Pack ID and proposed Concept Key.
4. Run the Concept Key check against the hidden Content Registry.
5. Define the intended launch assets and reserve their distinct Hook Keys.
6. Run Hook Key checks before moving assets beyond `Options ready`.
7. Advance only assets with complete, reviewed briefs. Unused assets remain in `Research backlog`.

## Lean-launch architecture

Week 01 has four mandatory anchor assets and up to four conditional extensions. Eight assets are a ceiling, not a production obligation.

| Channel | Mandatory anchor | Conditional extension |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | `HN-W01-LI-CAR-01` — application-autopsy document carousel | `HN-W01-LI-TXT-01` — evidence-led text or collaborative post |
| YouTube | `HN-W01-YT-LONG-01` — human-narrated 8–12 minute decision guide | `HN-W01-YT-SHORT-01` — short focused on one decisive clause or misunderstanding |
| Instagram | `HN-W01-IG-REEL-01` — fast role-verdict Reel | `HN-W01-IG-CAR-01` — saveable decision-checklist carousel |
| X | `HN-W01-X-THREAD-01` — sourced short thread | `HN-W01-X-SHOT-02` — annotated source or job-description screenshot |

An extension proceeds only when it has a distinct Hook Key, a format-native purpose, a non-duplicative argument and enough production capacity for the same evidence and trust review as an anchor. Otherwise it stays in `Research backlog`.

## Channel designs

### LinkedIn

Purpose: build professional trust through visible evidence and a clear applicant decision.

Anchor workflow:

1. Identify the exact role, route or source wording that changes the application decision.
2. Build an application autopsy covering eligibility, contractor structure, decisive evidence, caveats and the final applicant verdict.
3. Use restrained visual hierarchy, source excerpts and decision logic; avoid generic career-advice slides and stock office imagery.
4. Write a LinkedIn-native hook and caption after the evidence sequence is stable.
5. Add a product CTA only if a verified HireNudge capability is a natural next step.
6. Complete claim, accessibility, natural-language and trust review.

The conditional text or collaborative post must contribute a separate professional observation. Any expert contribution requires approval and accurate attribution. The post must not summarise the carousel slide by slide.

### YouTube

Purpose: build searchable authority by answering the complete applicant decision.

Anchor workflow:

1. Frame one high-intent question about whether and how an Indian fresher can pursue the selected opportunity or route.
2. Separate confirmed source facts, HireNudge analysis and unresolved uncertainty.
3. Explain the role, route, restrictions, contractor implications, decision path and practical verification steps.
4. Create a timecoded visual plan using primary-source screens, annotated wording and simple decision graphics.
5. Record with human narration in natural Indian professional English.
6. Verify that the title, thumbnail promise, script and conclusion all answer the same question.
7. Complete source, route, read-aloud and trust review before approval.

The conditional Short must stand alone around one decisive clause or misconception. It must not be a contextless extract from the long video.

### Instagram

Purpose: provide a fast role verdict and a saved reference for applicants.

Anchor workflow:

1. Open with the applicant decision rather than a motivational or fear-based hook.
2. Show the exact evidence early enough that viewers can understand the verdict.
3. Explain one caveat or restriction in plain language.
4. End with the next verification action; use a product CTA only when it is genuinely useful.
5. Use human narration, readable on-screen text and accessible captions.
6. Complete claim, source, natural-language and visual-rights review.

The conditional carousel must function as a decision checklist, verification sequence or saveable reference. It must not transcribe the Reel.

### X

Purpose: contribute sourced observations and create useful professional conversation with recruiters, remote employers and operators.

Anchor workflow:

1. Lead with the sourced observation or eligibility distinction.
2. Link or identify the primary source.
3. Explain what the evidence means for an Indian fresher and what it does not prove.
4. Keep the thread concise and invite informed correction or additional operator context where appropriate.
5. Avoid engagement bait, unsupported generalisation and product insertion unrelated to the evidence.
6. Complete claim, source and Hook Key review.

The conditional screenshot must annotate the exact source wording used in the conclusion and include sufficient context to avoid misrepresentation.

## Product-module reference rule

The HireNudge module inventory is a content-creation reference, not a coverage quota. Relevant product areas in the workbook include job matching and fit explanations, Nudge Studio and application tailoring, email outreach, job tracking, interview preparation, the Dashboard and Nudge assistant, and trust topics such as job freshness and responsible AI.

For every asset, choose one of three treatments:

- demonstrate a verified module when it materially strengthens the applicant decision;
- use a light product CTA when the module supplies a natural next step; or
- omit the product when the evidence-led content is stronger independently.

Before any product demonstration or capability claim, confirm whether the capability is verified live, in transition or proposed. Never present workbook recommendations as shipped functionality.

## CTA policy

The current production briefs contain the same default CTA on every channel. Week 01 will not force that CTA into every asset.

- Evidence and trust come before conversion.
- Product CTAs may repeat only when accurate and appropriate.
- Non-product CTAs may ask the audience to verify a restriction, save a checklist, watch the full explanation or contribute informed context.
- The final CTA for each asset is recorded in its production brief and reviewed alongside the hook and argument.

## Reviews and failure handling

Every produced asset must pass:

- source and claim review;
- remote or contractor-route accuracy review;
- consent and privacy review when a case, recruiter contribution, voice or likeness is involved;
- product-capability verification when HireNudge appears;
- channel-native language and duplicate review;
- read-aloud review for narration or spoken copy;
- accessibility and visual-rights review; and
- editor, trust and final approval.

Move an asset to `Hold` when the role closes before verification, source wording changes materially, location or contractor eligibility remains ambiguous, a product capability cannot be confirmed, consent is incomplete, or production quality is insufficient. Do not fill the gap with generic advice.

## Success measures

Week 01 will be evaluated primarily for trust signals rather than raw reach.

Primary indicators:

- saves, shares and substantive comments;
- returning viewers or readers;
- questions that show the applicant decision was understood; and
- informed engagement from recruiters, remote-work operators or career professionals.

Secondary indicators:

- qualified visits to HireNudge;
- role-analysis or other relevant product actions where tracking is available; and
- completion rate or watch time for video assets.

Follower growth and total impressions are diagnostic measures, not the main success criteria.

## Implementation boundary

The next execution phase is research, not production copy. It will:

1. find and verify the three required evidence inputs;
2. populate the Week 01 Research and Selection Doc;
3. present the comparison to Priyansh; and
4. stop for selection.

Only after selection and duplicate checks will the four anchor briefs be developed. Conditional extensions are evaluated after the anchors pass review.
